I picked up running in the summer following my first year as a way to destress while studying for my comprehensive exam. That same summer, I signed up for the Santa Monica 5k as a way to force myself to have a goal outside of passing my exam. Since then, I’ve run more races, partly to give me a goal outside of school, partly to stay healthy, and a huge part because I like collecting shiny medals and buying colorful shoes.
